ORBITREACH DIGITAL WEBSITE — STARTER PACKAGE

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html       Home page
- about.html       About page
- services.html    Services detail page
- portfolio.html   Portfolio / case-study framework
- insights.html    Insights page with starter articles
- contact.html     Contact form and FAQ
- privacy.html     Privacy policy starter template
- 404.html         Not-found page
- assets/          Shared stylesheet, JavaScript, config, favicon
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml

VIEW LOCALLY
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Use the menu to navigate between pages.

BEFORE PUBLISHING — IMPORTANT
1. BRAND: ORBITREACH DIGITAL is a temporary brand name. Replace it throughout the HTML if needed.
2. EMAIL: Open assets/config.js and change hello@yourdomain.com to your real business email.
   Also replace the placeholder email in contact.html, privacy.html, and the footer in the shared HTML pages.
3. DOMAIN: Replace https://www.yourdomain.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your actual domain.
4. CONTENT: Add your real business address/service area, team details, social profile links, and genuine contact details.
5. PORTFOLIO: Replace the sample capability cards with approved client work and verified results. No fabricated statistics or testimonials are included.
6. PRIVACY: privacy.html is a general starter, not legal advice. Adapt it to your real data practices and applicable law.
7. CONTACT FORM: The form validates in the browser and opens the visitor's email application with a pre-filled email. It does not submit to a server or CRM. For a production website, connect it to a trusted form service or your own backend and add spam protection.
8. HOSTING: Upload the extracted folder contents to the public web root of your hosting provider (often named public_html, www, or htdocs). Keep the assets folder in place.

TECHNOLOGY
- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step or framework required.
- Google Fonts are loaded online; system fonts are used as fallbacks.
- Designed to be responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

This package is a customizable website starter, not a hosted/published website. A domain and hosting account are needed to make it publicly available.

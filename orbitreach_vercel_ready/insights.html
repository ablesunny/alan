SEO fundamentals

SEO starts with understanding the searcher.

Search optimization is not simply a checklist of keywords. It is the work of making your pages helpful, clear, technically accessible, and aligned with what people are trying to accomplish.

Start by identifying the questions your audience asks at each stage. Review whether your pages answer those questions directly. Then prioritize technical improvements and content updates that make the experience easier for both people and search engines.

Social media

A social strategy is more than a posting schedule.

A useful social strategy defines who you are speaking to, what you want to be known for, and what role each channel plays. Build a small set of content themes your team can sustain, then adapt the format to fit the platform and audience.

Consistency matters, but so does listening. Use comments, questions, saves, and conversations to understand what resonates and where your audience needs more clarity.

Campaign measurement

Make campaign measurement more meaningful.

Before launching a campaign, define the business question it should help answer. Select a small set of measures that reflect that question, agree how they will be tracked, and document the baseline.

Look beyond surface-level activity. Interpret performance in context, note limitations, and use the learning to decide what to test next.

These are introductory educational overviews, not guarantees of outcomes. Your strategy should reflect your market, audience, and business constraints.
